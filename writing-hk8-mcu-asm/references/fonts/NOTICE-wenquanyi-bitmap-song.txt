WenQuanYi Bitmap Song 16px subset notice

This directory contains two mechanically extracted subsets of WenQuanYi Bitmap
Song 0.9.9.8 Release 8, both taken from the same upstream BDF by ENCODING with
bitmap bytes, DWIDTH, and BBX kept byte-identical to upstream:

1. wenquanyi_bitmap_song_16px_ascii_date_cn.bdf
   Printable ASCII (U+0020..U+007E) plus U+5E74, U+6708, U+53F7, U+4E2D,
   U+56FD, and U+FFE5. 101 glyphs.
   SHA256: 7cdde010b3d72a116c6f7058ce79c79d7e49e1a117d5a6a44453b9d2e65b9993

2. wenquanyi_bitmap_song_16px_gb2312.bdf
   Printable ASCII plus every GB2312 double-byte character present upstream,
   covering GB2312 levels 1 and 2 and the fullwidth punctuation block.
   7539 glyphs. U+2015 is absent from upstream and therefore absent here.
   SHA256: 27986209393675dcf4755a1e6cecfb548a81ead9fa1d3a7403511a3f59542000

Both hashes are registered in APPROVED_FONTS in scripts/bdf_to_ssd1306.py.
Text assets may only be generated from a registered font, and the release gate
re-verifies the bundled file's hash against that registration.
